3D rasmlar uchun promptlar

Ichida 19 ta rasm bor. Har birining kodi, ilovadagi joyi, rangi va inglizcha prompti berilgan: nusxa olib, AI generatorga qo‘yasiz. Barcha rasmlar bitta «oila»dek ko‘rinishi kerak: bir xil material, yorug‘lik va kamera burchagi.

Qanday ishlash kerak

  1. Faqat T01 dan boshlang. 5–10 ta variant yasang, eng yaxshisini tanlab menga yuboring. Uslubni shu rasmda birga tasdiqlaymiz.
  2. Tasdiqlangan T01 uslub namunasi bo‘ladi. Qolgan hamma rasmni shu rasmga tayanib yasaysiz (pastdagi «Bir xillik» bo‘limiga qarang).
  3. Har bir promptning boshiga MASTER STYLE matnini qo‘ying, oxiriga esa rasmning o‘z promptini.
  4. Avval P1 (11 ta) rasmlarni yasang, keyin P2 va P3.
  5. Fonni olib tashlang va pastdagi «Topshirish» talablari bo‘yicha saqlang.

MASTER STYLE — har promptning boshiga

3D render of a single isolated object, cute friendly rounded shapes, soft matte clay-like plastic material with subtle glossy highlights, smooth bevelled edges, pastel color palette, soft diffused studio lighting from the top-left, gentle ambient occlusion, soft contact shadow directly below the object, three-quarter view from slightly above (about 30 degrees), object centered with generous empty space around it, plain pure white background, clean minimal premium mobile app illustration style, high detail, sharp focus, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark

Negative prompt (agar generator «negative» maydonini qo‘llasa)

text, letters, words, cyrillic, latin, numbers, logo, watermark, signature, photorealistic photo, human hands, busy background, gradient background, multiple objects scattered, harsh shadows, dark mood, noise, blurry, cropped object, cut off edges

Bir xillik — eng muhim qoida

Midjourney

Har promptning oxiriga quyidagini qo‘shing:

--ar 1:1 --style raw --stylize 150
--sref [tasdiqlangan T01 havolasi]
--sw 200

Bu yerda --sref uslubni T01 dan oladi. Rang yoki obyekt T01 dan ko‘chib o‘tsa, --sw ni 100 ga tushiring.

ChatGPT / boshqa rasm modellari

T01 rasmini biriktiring va promptdan oldin shuni yozing:

Use the attached image ONLY as a style reference: same material, lighting, camera angle, level of detail and soft shadow. Create a NEW object described below. Transparent background if possible.
Nima uchun harf yo‘q? AI generatorlar kirill harflarini deyarli har doim buzadi: «Я» o‘rniga «Ꞩ» chiqadi va hokazo. Shuning uchun obyektlarda yozuv bo‘lmaydi. Kerak bo‘lsa, harfni men kodda, rasm ustiga qo‘yaman.
